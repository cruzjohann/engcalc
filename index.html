Constant rationale:
(1) SL, VCJ, σ — stoichiometric worst-case; actual purge mixtures leaner/richer → longer XDDT → conservative bound.
(2) ν varies ±5% over 280–320 K at 1 atm → negligible effect on Re and De.
(3) BR = 0.1 — conservative for minor internal fittings; higher BR reduces XDDT further.
(4) LFL/UFL (5%/15%) — methane limits at 1 atm ambient (NFPA 54, GPA); temperature sensitivity <0.5 pp over operating range.

Silvestrini et al. (2008), Basha & Malaeb (2007), AGA Purging Principles and Practice.

[X/D]_DDT = 1/(0.006·(σ−1)) · (0.15/D)^0.4 · ln(0.077·V_CJ/(σ·S_L)) · 1/(1+15·BR) [Eq 1a] X_DDT = [X/D]_DDT · D [Eq 1b] L_flam = A_eff · sqrt(D_e · t), where t = L_pipe / V_slug [Eq 2d] A_eff = 0.1849 × 2.18 = 0.4031 (LFL–UFL fraction of erf profile) [Eq 2c] L_safe = X_DDT² · V_slug / (A_eff² · D_e) [Eq 2e] log10[(D_e/(V_purge·d) − 0.03)/0.1] = (68 + 18·((Re−1000)/1000)^−3) · exp(−log10(Re)^1.1) [Eq 3] Re = V_purge · d / ν V_purge = 0.843 · D^0.508 ft/s (D in inches) [Eq 4a] V_slug = 52 · √D ft/min [Eq 4b]
Criterion: Lflam < XDDT → direct purge safe. Lflam ≥ XDDT → nitrogen inert slug required. At Lpipe = Lsafe, Lflam = XDDT exactly (boundary — slug required).
Screening tool only. Does not replace QRA or compliance with ASME B31.8, IGE/TD/1, AGA Purging Principles and Practice, or operator-specific procedures. Valid for natural gas (predominantly methane) transmission pipelines, 6″–48″, near-atmospheric pressure purging with air.
